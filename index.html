Notebook Page 2 of 10

Vocabulary: Words to Know (pp. 48–63)

6 Official Terms

1. Charter

Textbook: A formal document issued by the king that outlined a colony's geographic boundaries and specified how it would be governed.

✍️ Aaron's words: "a document that maps out an area"

2. Mayflower Compact

Textbook: An agreement that Pilgrims wrote and signed describing how they would govern themselves in the Americas.

✍️ Aaron's words: "an agreement stating how the Pilgrims would govern themselves"

3. Democratic

Textbook: A form of government where people have the power to rule, vote, or elect representatives.

✍️ Aaron's words: "a form of government where people can vote"

4. Cash Crop

Textbook: A crop raised in large quantities and sold for commercial profit (tobacco, rice, indigo).

✍️ Aaron's words: "a plant sold for profit"

5. Mercantilism

Textbook: An economic policy in which nations tried to gain wealth and power by controlling trade and establishing colonies.

✍️ Aaron's words: "they did things to try and make more money"

6. Slave Trade

Textbook: The business of capturing, transporting, and selling people as slaves across the Atlantic.

✍️ Aaron's words: "people buying slaves and bringing them to America"

Notebook Page 4 of 10

Student Sample: South Carolina

Southern Model

Founders: Eight English nobles (the Lords Proprietors) — to establish an agricultural base.

Settlers: Colonists from England; later, colonists from Barbados.

Climate: Mild winters and humid summers.

Geography: Swamps and coastline.

Economy / Jobs: Agricultural, based on rice and indigo.

Religion: All faiths.

Government: Self-governing.

📌 Post-It Method Facts:

1. Charles Town (today's Charleston) became a busy port city.

2. Enslaved Africans brought rice-growing skills and did most of the plantation work.

🌾 Symbol Drawn: Rice plant (rice = main crop)
Notebook Page 5 of 10

Section 2: Massachusetts & Section 3: Rhode Island

New England

Massachusetts (p. 51)

⛵

Founders: Pilgrims led by William Bradford (1620) & Puritans led by John Winthrop (1630).

Settlers: Puritans seeking religious freedom.

Climate: Hard winters, warm summers.

Geography: Sandy coast with good ports, rich pastures, forests.

Economy: Farming, lumbering, shops, shipping.

Government: Self-governed (Mayflower Compact, town meetings).

📌 Post-It 1: The Pilgrims signed the Mayflower Compact to govern themselves.

📌 Post-It 2: The Wampanoag taught the Pilgrims to grow crops, hunt, and fish.

🎨 Symbol: Mayflower ship

Rhode Island (p. 52)

⛪

Founders: Roger Williams and Anne Hutchinson.

Settlers: People seeking religious freedom from strict Puritans.

Climate: Hot humid summers, cold snowy winters.

Geography: Coastal lowlands; flat, rocky woodlands.

Economy: Farming, ships, fishing, whaling, trade.

Religion: Various faiths — everyone welcomed!

Government: Self-governed.

📌 Post-It 1: Williams founded Providence in 1636.

📌 Post-It 2: Rhode Island was a slave trading center.

🎨 Symbol: Church

Notebook Page 6 of 10

Section 4: Connecticut & Section 5: New York

NE & Middle

Connecticut (p. 53)

🌳

Founders: Thomas Hooker.

Settlers: Puritans seeking a new settlement.

Climate: Cold winters, mild summers.

Geography: Forested hills, sea coast.

Economy: Farming, ships, fishing, whaling.

Government: Self-governing; first written constitution in the colonies, "The Fundamental Orders".

📌 Post-It 1: Founded in 1639.

📌 Post-It 2: Charter Oak symbolized freedom.

🎨 Symbol: Charter Oak tree

New York (p. 54)

⛵

Founders: Dutch West India Company (1624); James, Duke of York (1664).

Settlers: Dutch and English.

Climate: Cold winters, hot summers.

Geography: Wetlands, Hudson River, mountains.

Economy: Farming, trading, shipping, mining.

Government: British appointed a governor, along with an elected assembly.

📌 Post-It 1: English took over in 1664 without firing a shot.

📌 Post-It 2: Assembly granted in 1683.

🎨 Symbol: Trading ship / boat

Notebook Page 7 of 10

Section 6: Pennsylvania & Section 7: Maryland

Middle & Southern

Pennsylvania (p. 55)

🕊️

Founders: William Penn — King Charles II owed Penn's father money and repaid the debt with American land (glad to send Quaker troublemakers away!).

Settlers: Quakers and Europeans.

Climate: Cold winters; hot, humid summers.

Geography: Rolling hills, trees, and fertile soil.

Economy: Farming, lumbering, ships, grain.

Religion: Quakers; various faiths — all are welcome!

Government: Self-governed.

📌 Post-It 1: Founded in 1681.

📌 Post-It 2: Quakers believed in equality.

🎨 Symbol: Dove of peace

Maryland (p. 56)

✝️

Founders: Cecil Calvert (Second Lord Baltimore).

Settlers: Catholics and Protestants seeking religious and political freedom.

Climate: Cold winters, hot summers.

Geography: Low, fertile land surrounding the Chesapeake Bay.

Economy: Farming (tobacco), fishing, shipping.

Religion: Catholic & Protestant.

Government: Self-governing; assemblies of elected officials.

📌 Post-It 1: Founded in 1634.

📌 Post-It 2: Religious freedom law in 1649 (Toleration Act).

🎨 Symbol: Christian cross

Notebook Page 8 of 10

Section 8: Virginia & Section 9: Georgia

Southern Colonies

Virginia (p. 57)

🌿

Founders: Walter Raleigh, Virginia Company.

Settlers: English workers and landowners.

Climate: Mild winters; hot, humid summers.

Geography: Wooded mountains, coastal plain.

Economy: Farming (tobacco) that relied on slave labor, plantations, small farms.

Religion: Church of England (Anglican).

Government: House of Burgesses passed a law in 1661 making African workers slaves for life.

📌 Post-It 1: Jamestown founded in 1607.

📌 Post-It 2: House of Burgesses formed in 1619.

🎨 Symbol: Tobacco plant ("tobaco")

Georgia (p. 59)

🛡️

Founders: James Oglethorpe and King George II (wanted a buffer against Spanish Florida).

Settlers: Debtors, Europeans.

Climate: Short mild winters; long, hot, humid summers.

Geography: Wetlands, red-clay plains, forested mountains.

Economy: Farming, trade.

Religion: Various faiths. Oglethorpe imagined a utopian society with laws against drinking and owning slaves.

Government: Self-governed.

📌 Post-It 1: Founded in 1732.

📌 Post-It 2: Last of the 13 colonies.

🎨 Symbol: Protective shield
